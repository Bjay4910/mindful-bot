How it works

Three AI agents working together

mindful-bot uses a pipeline of specialised AI agents to ensure you always learn, never just copy.

The agents

1

Main Chatbot — Socratic Method

Handles all conversation using the Socratic method. When you ask something, it asks you back: "What do you already know?" It refuses to hand out answers until you've demonstrated effort. Powered by claude-sonnet-4-6.

2

Recall Challenge Generator

After every 3 exchanges, this agent analyses your conversation and generates a targeted recall question — something specific and testable from what you just discussed. Returns structured JSON with question, hint, and correct answer.

3

Evaluator Agent

Scores your recall answers 0–10 with specific, constructive feedback. Neither too harsh nor too generous. Returns your score, what you got right, what you missed, and the full correct answer.

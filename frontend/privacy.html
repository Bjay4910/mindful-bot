Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

What we collect

We collect your email address and username when you create an account. We also store the messages you send to the chatbot and your recall challenge answers in order to provide the service.

How we use your data

Your conversation data is used solely to power the AI chatbot and generate recall challenges. We do not sell your data to third parties. Anonymised, aggregated data may be used to improve the service.

Third-party services

We use Supabase for authentication and database storage, and the Anthropic Claude API to generate AI responses. Your messages are sent to Anthropic's API for processing. Please review Anthropic's privacy policy for details on how they handle data.

Data retention

Your account data is retained while your account is active. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us at gyampoh.b.james@gmail.com.

Security

All data is stored in Supabase with Row Level Security enabled. API communications use HTTPS. We never store plaintext passwords — authentication is handled by Supabase Auth.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email gyampoh.b.james@gmail.com.

About mindful-bot

mindful-bot is a cognitive offload resistance chatbot built as a project for an Intelligent Systems course. The core idea is simple: most AI tools encourage you to offload your thinking to the machine. mindful-bot does the opposite — it refuses to hand you answers until you've made a genuine attempt to recall or reason first.

The problem it solves

When you Google something or ask ChatGPT, you get the answer immediately. This is convenient but terrible for learning. Research consistently shows that active recall — trying to remember before checking — dramatically improves long-term retention compared to passive reading or being told answers.

mindful-bot uses the Socratic method: every question you ask is met with a question back. After a few exchanges, a recall challenge appears testing what you've just discussed. You earn points for correct answers and build streaks for consistent performance.
